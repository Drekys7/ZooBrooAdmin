# Aquarium and terrarium quick facts

Updated 2026-09-24. Values are educational species-level approximations, not measurements of the zoo's own animals. Longevity maxima may refer to animals in human care. Female tarantula longevity is explicitly marked ♀; males typically live much less long. Existing habitat regions are retained. Both languages and the three existing fact icons are stored in the content, rather than hard-coded into the client UI.

| Species | Sources and editorial interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Amphiprion ocellaris | [National Zoo & Aquarium](https://www.nationalzoo.com.au/clownfish): captive longevity includes 20 years. Its 200 g weight is not used; [breeding study](https://openurl.ebsco.com/contentitem/gcd%3A47389432?crl=f&id=ebsco%3Agcd%3A47389432&jrnl=00113891&sid=ebsco%3Aplink%3Acrawler-gcd) reports spawning adults of 14.6–15 g, displayed as approximately 15 g. |
| Zebrasoma flavescens | [ADW](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Zebrasoma_flavescens/): maximum 30 years in the wild. Weight is an **estimate** for 10–20 cm fish using [FishBase's length–weight relationship](https://fishbase.se/popdyn/FishLWSummary.php?ID=6018&fc=412&genusname=Zebrasoma&gm_a=0.026409190275714&gm_b=3.0264293554897&id2=7811&speciesname=flavescens&variable_Length=10), W = 0.02640919 × L^3.026429 (grams, centimetres), rounded to approximately 30–230 g; not a measured species-wide range. |
| Pterois volitans | [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/lionfish.htm): 15 years. [NOAA study](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/50712): specimens up to 1153 g, rounded to approximately 1.2 kg. |
| Hippocampus kuda | [Dimensions](https://www.dimensions.com/element/common-seahorse-hippocampus-kuda): approximate 1–4 years. Weight intentionally marked not established: no defensible general adult weight range was verified; individual-study or juvenile measurements must not be presented as a species-wide range. |
| Pterapogon kauderni | [NOAA](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/banggai-cardinalfish): maximum 5 years. [FishBase](https://fishbase.se/popdyn/PopCharSummary.php?GenusName=Pterapogon&ID=15426&SpeciesName=kauderni&autoctr=16101&fc=304&vStockcode=14687): Wmax 11.30 g, rounded. |
| Timon lepidus | [Wildwood](https://kent.wildwoodtrust.org/plan-your-visit/animals/ocellated-lizard/): 20 years in captivity. [TunisieVet](https://tunisievet.com/en/animalia/animalpedia/lezard-ocelle): approximate 200–500 g. |
| Eublepharis macularius | [Amnéville Zoo](https://zoo-amneville.com/en/leopard-gecko/): approximately 80 g, longevity up to 20 years. |

| Tliltocatl albopilosus | [Niabi Zoo fact sheet](https://www.niabizoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Curly-Haired-Tarantula.pdf): females up to 30 years in zoos and 2 oz, converted and rounded to 57 g. |
| Chromatopelma cyaneopubescens | [Josh's Frogs](https://training.joshsfrogs.com/sp/green-bottle-blue-tarantula---chromatopelma-cyaneopubescens---3-4-inch--captive-bred--jftchrocya4): females 12–14 years. | Caribena versicolor | [Scuttle & Squeak breeder care guide](https://scuttleandsqueak.com/care-guides/tarantulas/caribena-versicolor): females 12–15 years. | Lasiodora parahybana | [Bicton College](https://bicton.ac.uk/animal-zone/our-animals/chilean-rose-tarantula/): body explicitly identifies L. parahybana despite incorrect page heading, 12–15 years and females over 100 g.
